(2) The CoreWeave callout credits the 100bps May to August step to timing alone. CoreWeave itself says DDTL 5.5 is backed by shorter contracts. (3) The Meta callout says investors price "structure". The page 6 paragraph says the two deals share the same template. The main unverifiable items are the following. "June 2029" rent start appears only in an aggregator, though the year 2029 is confirmed. The Aligned "caps senior notes at 70%" may be S&P wording from an earlier Aligned series. ## Internal consistency and arithmetic

- **Hyperion RVG.** "About $28bn" matches in page1.p3, the table ("~$28bn RVG") and callL. Against $27.3bn of bonds, the arithmetic is correct. The $28bn is an aggregate threshold with no liability recorded because payment is "not probable". "The guarantee totals" (callL) could read "is capped at about $28bn". This is optional.
- **Hyperion maturity 2049 and El Paso 2048.** These match across page1.p1, page1.p3, the table, page2.p2 and callL.
- **El Paso against Hyperion.** 287.5 minus 225 = 62.5bps, consistent in page2.p1 and callL. Comparing spreads is the right basis. The chart C footnote now says so.
- **Cover ratios.** Apollo's figures cover hyperscaler bonds. Bloomberg's "close to four times" covers all bond sales this year, as of late July. Both sit in one sentence of page2.p1 with different scopes. The wording ("the average bond sale") makes this clear enough. The 1.6x for El Paso: 12.5 x 1.6 = 20. Correct.
- **CoreWeave gap.** 550 minus 225 = 325. The scope is now "A3 and Ba2 facilities", which is correct.
- **Oracle rating.** BBB- in the table (Jupiter row) and page3.p1. Consistent.
- **Saline.** 14 / 16.3 = 85.9%. The table note says LTC is "derived from the reported 15% equity". Strictly it is derived from $14bn of bonds against $16.3bn of cost, which gives 14.1% equity. Optional rewording.
- **Table "Final" column.** It mixes calendar years (2042 to 2049, 2030 to 2032) with tenors (5 yr, 5 yr ARD, 10 yr ARD). All values are correct. BX VLT10 also has a 20 year legal final, and Aligned a longer legal final, which the ARD label covers. A layout or style note only.
