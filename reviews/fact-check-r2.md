I checked 58 items. **49 are confirmed, 6 conflict and 3 are unverifiable.** Most of the Round 1 fixes landed correctly. These now check out: the Morgan Stanley wording, the Reuters lease wording on Oracle, the S&P "widening cash flow deficit", the Hyperion RVG (16 years), the Moody's four to six years, the Hyperion spread of about 225bps, and the El Paso size, coupon and amortization. 1. **Chart D is mislabeled and should go back to verified false.** Meta's own El Paso release says those leases also have a four year initial term, with four extension options for a potential 20 years. The "El Paso lease 20" bar therefore sets a potential term next to Hyperion's committed initial term.
2. **The August high yield line is contradicted.** Two manager reports put August 2026 high yield issuance near $27bn, which they describe as a rebound from a 15 month low. The draft says it was a one year low of $12bn.
3. **The Blue Owl halt was announced on February 18, 2026, not in March.**
4. **The El Paso orders figure is disputed.** Bloomberg says demand "reached $20bn on Friday" during marketing, but another report gives final subscriptions of about $17bn. My Round 1 fix was too firm.
5. **"Meta's El Paso bonds" names the wrong issuer.** The issuer is BlackRock sponsored Sopaipilla Investor LLC.
6. **"Duration" cannot explain El Paso's wider spread.** El Paso has the shorter final maturity (2048 against 2049).
7. **Chart A still holds the unverified Oracle 6.5%** and stays verified false. This one is unverifiable, not a conflict.
